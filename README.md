# 📼 Sunset Pixels

A chunky 16-bit vintage cassette Walkman music player for Android that combines **local music files** with **Spotify**.

## ✨ Features

- **16-Bit Vintage Walkman UI:** Distressed burnt-orange chassis, warm tape-window lamp, stepped mechanical reel animation, and a mint-green dot-matrix LCD screen.
- **Physical Cassette Collection:**
  - **Artist Shelf:** Automatically generates artist and album cassettes with pixelated 16-bit cover art on the spines.
  - **Playlist Shoe Box:** Homemade mixtape cassettes with handwritten-style labels.
- **Tactile Mechanical Controls:** Chunky transport buttons (Skip, Rewind, Play, Fast-Forward, Dynamic Stop/Eject) and Shuffle/Repeat rocker switches with trimmed mechanical button audio & haptics.
- **Spotify + Local Hybrid Playback:** Log in with Spotify (OAuth 2.0 PKCE) to import your Spotify playlists, Liked Songs, and Saved Albums directly onto your tape shelves alongside local audio files.
